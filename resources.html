Resources

Practical guidance pulled from the live Granny Flats, Building Approvals and CDC / DA pages.

The original site carries useful planning information, but it was buried in the broader one-page rebuild. It now has its own destination so clients can focus on early-stage guidance before they enquire.

Planning pathways

What the live site keeps emphasising.

  • Projects can move from CDC to DA where complying development is not possible.
  • Approval-ready plans and supporting documents are a major part of the service mix.
  • New homes and renovations need approval before work starts.
  • Granny flat feasibility depends on lot area, zoning, width and setbacks.

First-hour topics

Useful questions to bring into the first consultation.

  • Is the project likely to go through CDC or DA?
  • What site constraints affect layout, approval timing or design ambition?
  • Which spaces need 3D concept work before documentation begins?
  • What level of builder, consultant and certifier coordination will be needed?
