Approvals support

DA, CDC and building approval guidance without unnecessary complexity.

When a Complying Development Certificate is not possible, the path shifts to a Development Application. Green Design Sydney's current copy emphasises balancing cost control with enough information for councils and certifiers to approve confidently.

  • Planning and building controls reviewed early
  • Statement of Environmental Effects support where appropriate
  • Documentation coordinated before works begin
